Privacy Policy

Effective 11/11/2026

MyStoryChip respects your privacy. This page explains in plain English what we collect, why, who helps us run the service, and how we protect it.

We never sell, rent or trade your information. No advertising. No tracking tools.

1.What we collect

  • When you buy: your name and email. Payments are handled by Stripe — we never see or store your card number.
  • Your story page: the title, story, photos, documents and contact details you choose to add.
  • Your PIN: stored only in scrambled form. Not even we can read it.
  • Your chip: its chip code and the chip's tag ID.
  • Basic website logs our hosting company keeps to run and protect the site.

2.How we use it

To create and run your story page, send your setup link, PIN resets and receipts, answer your questions, prevent misuse, and keep required business records.

3.MyStoryChip is not social media

There are no followers, no feeds, no likes and no ads. Your page isn't connected to Facebook or any other social network, and we never share your information with them. Your page is seen only by people who tap your chip or have its link — or, if it's private, only by someone with your PIN. Our story pages are also marked so search engines like Google don't list them.

4.Who can see your page

  • Public: anyone who taps your chip or has its link.
  • Private: only someone with your PIN.
  • You can also mark single photos private.

Please choose carefully what you share.

5.Your private content is locked behind your PIN

Our website and admin tools can't open a private page without your PIN, and we don't keep your PIN — only a scrambled version no one can read. Our technical systems can be reached by MyStoryChip's owner for maintenance and backups, but we never use that to look at your private content unless you ask us to, or the law requires it.

6.Companies that help us

  • Stripe — payments
  • Vercel — website and photo storage
  • Neon — database
  • Resend — email

Each gets only what it needs to do its job.

7.Cookies

One small cookie keeps you signed in while you edit your page. No advertising cookies.

8.How we protect it

  • Secure (HTTPS) connections
  • Scrambled PINs
  • Automatic lockout after repeated wrong PINs
  • A password-protected admin area
  • Payment keys limited to only what the site needs

9.Keeping and deleting

Your page stays for the life of your MyStoryChip, until you ask us to close it. A chip that sits unused for a while doesn't delete anything. You can remove photos any time. To close your page and delete your information, contact us and we'll remove it — except records the law requires us to keep (like purchase records).

10.When a chip changes hands

If a chip is passed on — for example a promo chip that's claimed, or an item that's sold with its chip — the page and everything on it pass to the new owner. Remove anything you want to keep private before handing a chip over.

11.If something ever goes wrong

If your information is ever exposed in a security incident, we'll tell you promptly and explain what we're doing about it.

12.Requests from the law

We share information with authorities only when the law requires it, such as a valid court order.

13.Where your information is kept

Your information is stored in the United States.

14.Children

MyStoryChip isn't meant for children under 13 to buy or run on their own, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

15.Changes

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here with a new date.
