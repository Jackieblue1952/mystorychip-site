The Story Behind MyStoryChip

Where memories live... beyond time.

It started with a quiet day.

While visiting family and friends at the cemetery, I found myself thinking about the lives behind the names. The stories. The memories. The experiences that once filled entire lifetimes — now reduced to a few words carved in stone.

As I drove through, one headstone stood out.
A familiar name.

A familiar face. Someone who had once made people laugh, live, build, love, and matter. Yet standing there, all that remained was a simple marker and a few small mementos. It didn’t come close to telling the full story of a life.

And that thought stayed with me:

How many stories are lost forever?

Not just famous ones — but everyday lives.
Family histories. Skills. Lessons. Memories.
Entire lifetimes... gone, with no way to be shared again.

Later that same day, I was at home working with my coin collection — scanning pieces, pulling up their history instantly on my phone.

That’s when it hit me.

Why can’t we do this for people... for stories... for real life?

What if a simple tap could open a life story?
What if a headstone could speak?
What if memories didn’t have to disappear?

That idea became something much bigger.

I began to see it everywhere.

  • A restored classic car, carrying its full history
  • A family recipe, preserved and shared for generations
  • A home project, showing the work behind the walls
  • A collection, telling the story behind every piece

Not just storage... but connection.

But there was a problem.

NFC chips themselves don’t hold much data. And using third-party platforms meant giving up control, privacy, and permanence.

That didn’t sit right.

So instead of relying on someone else’s system... I decided to build my own.

With guidance from a trusted friend, the backend was created — a secure system designed to store and serve each story. On my end, I learned, built, and shaped the experience from the ground up.

I moved away from traditional setups, installed Linux, and took full control of the environment.

And from that...

MyStoryChip.com was born.

What It Is Today

MyStoryChip is simple at its core:

One chip. One link. One place for what matters.

A way to connect real-world objects to the stories behind them — instantly, privately, and permanently.

Not social media.
Not noise.
Not something that gets buried or forgotten.

Just your story... accessible with a single tap.

Why It Matters

Because too much gets lost.

Boxes get packed away.
Photos fade.
Stories go untold.

And over time, even the most meaningful parts of life can disappear.

MyStoryChip was built to change that.

Your Chip. Your Story.
