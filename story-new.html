The Story Behind MyStoryChip

It started with a quiet day.

While visiting family and friends at the cemetery, I found myself thinking about the lives behind the names.

The stories. The memories.
Entire lifetimes — reduced to a few words carved in stone.

As I drove through, one headstone stood out.

A familiar name.

Someone who once made people laugh… now reduced to a simple marker.
A few dates. A few words.
Not the story of a life.

And it made me wonder…

How many stories are lost to time?

Not just the famous ones — but everyday lives.
Family histories. Skills. Lessons. Memories.
Entire lifetimes… gone.

That’s when it hit me.

What if a simple tap could bring it all back?

MyStoryChip.com was born.

One chip. One link. One place to preserve what matters.

Not just storage… but connection.

Your Chip. Your Story.
