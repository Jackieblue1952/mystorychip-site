What is MyStoryChip?

MyStoryChip is a physical NFC chip you attach to something meaningful so a person can tap it with their phone and instantly open the story behind it. It turns memory, meaning, and information into something simple to access and easy to share.

Why People Will Care

  • It makes a memory or object feel alive
  • It is simple enough for everyday people to use
  • It creates an instant emotional connection
  • It works for personal, collectible, and business uses

What They Get
